FIDO Help

FIDO helps you track freezer items, their owners, categories, and dates.

Inventory

  • Fresh shows items that have not reached their 30-day expiry date. Use Expired to see expired items.
  • Use the owner filters to see everyone’s items or one owner’s items. Sort by name, expiry date, or sell-by date.
  • Each item shows how many days it has been in the freezer and how many days remain until its sell-by date.
  • Use Quick Add to select a previous item and prefill the add form.

Settings

  • Add items, manage food categories, and export or import a JSON backup from the Settings tab.
  • Use Owner Names to change the labels shown for Hers, His, and Shared. These labels do not change item assignments.
  • Importing a backup replaces the current inventory, categories, and owner names when those names are included.

Offline and data

After opening FIDO online once, the app shell is cached for offline use. Inventory, categories, and owner names are stored in this browser. Export a backup regularly to avoid data loss.
